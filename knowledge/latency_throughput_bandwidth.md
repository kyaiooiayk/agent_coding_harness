# Latency vs Throughput vs Bandwidth
***

# What is this file about?
- This file explain briefly when to use Latency vs Throughput vs Bandwidth
***

## Latency
- It is the delay. How long it takes for a single packet to travel from sender to receiver. If your ping shows 40 ms round-trip, that's latency.
***

## Throughput
- It is the actual delivery rate. How much data is successfully transferred per second. If your download shows 62 Mbps, that’s throughput.
***

## Bandwidth
- It is the maximum capacity of the link. For example, a 100 Mbps connection is the upper limit under ideal conditions.
***

## Mental model
- Bandwidth is the highway width. Throughput is the traffic flow. Latency is how long it takes a car to go from A to B.
