# Authentication & authorization
***

## What are they?
Authentication: Verifies who you are (e.g., login with password, OAuth token, biometric check).
Authorization: Determines what you are allowed to do (e.g., access a resource, edit data, admin vs user permissions).
***

## Mental model
Imagine authentication as permission to enter a building. And authorization as the permission to enter specific rooms of the building.
***
